Core Platform Pillars

One platform, five pillars of service excellence

From real-time performance monitoring to predictive maintenance, service execution, self-guided knowledge, and e-commerce — iSERV connects every part of the aftermarket service journey into a single experience. Select a pillar to see what's inside.

Performance Monitoring

Securely connect to field assets, monitor real-time performance, and analyze operational trends.

  • Asset connectivity, proactive alerts & real-time dashboards
  • OEE Dashboard for root-cause analysis & quality assurance
  • Machine uptime analytics, downtime tracking & resource optimization
  • Persona-based performance insights & benchmarking

Predictive Maintenance

An AI-driven module delivering real-time, actionable insights to optimize efficiency and uncover patterns.

  • Digital logbook & automated inspection checklists
  • Work order scheduling & management
  • Condition-based maintenance
  • Schedule-based maintenance

Smart Service Application

An all-in-one Support + Operations experience built for an outstanding customer service journey.

  • Self-guided content, FAQs & how-to guides
  • Integrated ticketing & case management
  • Remote collaboration with AR/VR
  • Customer onboarding & technician scheduling/routing

Knowledge Portal

Fosters self-guided issue resolution and continuous training across your customer base.

  • Troubleshooting guides & FAQs for self-service
  • Content hub with document management
  • Seamless regulatory compliance
  • Training & learning board, how-to videos, schematics

E-Commerce Integration

End-to-end spare parts ordering with full ERP connectivity, from cart to delivery.

  • Searchable parts catalog with 2D/3D product views
  • ERP integration, BOM visualization & order tracking
  • One-click cart, flexible shipping & payment options
  • Live pricing, 24/7 support & automated order emails
