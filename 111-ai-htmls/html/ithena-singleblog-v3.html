FAQ

Questions people ask about Aether

Short answers to the questions that come up most often about the platform and the feature.

What is Aether?

Aether is ITHENA’s Agentic AI-powered advanced analytics platform. It combines Enterprise AI, Generative AI, Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation in one platform, so teams can move from a question to a decision, and from a decision to an action, without crossing a tool boundary.

How is Aether different from a traditional BI dashboard?

A traditional dashboard is built to describe what happened and stops there. Aether interprets the data, recommends what to do next, and can initiate the workflow, notification or operational action itself.

Can you ask Aether questions in plain English?

Yes. Questions asked in natural language return instant, contextual answers drawn from enterprise data - no query language, no report request and no queue - and where the answer implies an action, Aether can trigger it.

Which industries is Aether built for?

Aether is designed for data-intensive, time-sensitive operations, including manufacturing, supply chain, healthcare and finance.

Where was Aether featured?

In The Enterprise World, July 2026 edition, under the title “Talking to Your Data: How Aether Turns Enterprise Chaos into Clarity” - its second consecutive feature in the magazine.

What does Agentic AI mean in Aether?

Agentic AI recommends and initiates business actions from AI-generated insight, rather than leaving the follow-through to someone’s inbox.

The problem

The data exists. The decision still waits.

Enterprises are not short of dashboards, reports or real-time feeds. What is missing sits between the chart and the thing somebody does about it.

Conventional analytics platforms deliver a view and stop there. Interpreting that view, deciding what it means for the shift, the plant or the quarter, and then initiating something - all of it stays manual, and all of it stays with whoever happens to be looking at the screen.

That gap is where the July feature places the real cost of analytics. Every hour spent crossing it is an hour of operational efficiency, productivity and margin that the reporting layer has already been paid for and has not delivered.

It is also a gap most tools have by design. A dashboard is built to describe. Nothing inside it is responsible for the next step.

Beyond analytics

Built to act, not only to explain

Aether combines Enterprise AI, Generative AI, Agentic AI and Intelligent Automation in one platform, so the path from question to action does not cross a tool boundary.

A user asks in natural language. The platform answers with context drawn from enterprise data rather than a static report, and where the answer implies something should happen, it can trigger the workflow, the notification or the operational action itself.

That is the difference the July feature draws out. Traditional business intelligence describes what is happening. Aether is built to help an organisation determine what to do next, and then to do it.

The companies pulling ahead are not the ones with the most data. They are the ones who move fastest from data to decision, and from decision to action.
