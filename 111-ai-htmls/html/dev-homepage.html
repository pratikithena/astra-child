Questions

The things people ask us before the first call.

Answered from what the platform does today, not from a roadmap.

What does ITHENA actually do?
ITHENA is an industrial software and AI company. We connect the ERP, CRM, PLCs, MES and service desk you already run into one data layer, put practical AI on top of it, and hand each person the next thing to do through apps built for their role. Quotes, production, service and spares end up in one flow instead of six systems that never talk to each other.
Do we have to replace the systems we already run?
No. There is no rip and replace. ITHENA sits on top of what you own, with connectors for ERP, CRM, PLC, SCADA and MES out of the box, and it runs alongside Rockwell, PTC, Infor, Oracle and Microsoft. We map your real systems on site before we propose anything.
Where do we start?
With whichever of the three describes you. If you build equipment, we start on the front office and the aftermarket, one record per machine. If you run a plant, we start on the Gemba, the line and the energy bill. If you run the business across sites, we start on the data hub, analytics and the customer side. The starting point changes. The platform underneath does not.
Which industries do you work in?
Machine builders and distributors in packaging machinery, food and beverage equipment, compressed air, material handling and custom tool building. Plants in industrial and discrete manufacturing, automotive, life sciences, food and beverage, metals and energy and utilities. And enterprises in banking, financial services and insurance, retail and consumer goods, government and media.
What is Aether?
Aether is the AI platform inside ITHENA, not a chatbot bolted on. It reads what your ERP, CRM, PLCs and MES already produce, learns what normal looks like on your plant, flags a drift with a severity and a risk window, explains what changed in plain language, and can raise the ticket, reserve the spare and open the work order itself. Every recommendation carries its evidence and its confidence, so your engineers can challenge it.
Which products do you make?
Smart Service (iSERV), PanelOps industrial ERP, Digital Shopfloor (iGEMBA), Manufacturing Excellence (iMEX), Energy Management (iSEMS), B2B eCommerce, AI MRO and The Agentic OEM, plus iCRM, iCPQ, iHub and iSIP. They share the same data layer, the same AI layer and the same connectors, so you can start with the one you need and add the next without a second integration project.
How long before we see something working?
Weeks, not years. Pre built accelerators and pre built connectors carry the discovery, deployment and scale steps, so the first results land in weeks rather than at the end of a multi year programme.
Do you only sell software?
No. ITHENA also runs the work around it: advisory and AI strategy, big data and analytics, AI, machine learning and data science, cloud adoption and process integration, systems of engagement and customer experience, plant integration, manufacturing execution and industrial IoT. The people doing it have run production lines, service desks and ERP go lives.
What results have customers reported?
The numbers our customers reported on their own operational KPIs: 27 percent faster response to service calls at a packaging OEM on Smart Service, 40 percent boost in operational visibility and 30 percent faster BOM and parts management at an industrial automation firm on PanelOps, 22 percent increase in production quality at a discrete manufacturer on Aether, 38 percent higher customer satisfaction at a sampling and analysis OEM on iSERV, and a 15 percent rise in productivity in the process industry on Aether.
What happens in the 30 minute walkthrough?
No slide deck. You bring the process that hurts most, a quote that takes too long, a line you cannot see, or a fault that takes three visits, and an ITHENA specialist walks it through the platform on your own data. A specialist reaches out within one business day.
