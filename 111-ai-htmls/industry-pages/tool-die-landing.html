Custom Tooling Challenges

High-Precision, Low-Volume Realities

Every custom tool and die job is essentially one-of-a-kind. ITHENA's platform is built around the specific operational pain points that come with building, tracking, and servicing custom tooling at scale.

Precision + Low-Volume Production

Tight tolerances and small batches make standardized processes ineffective - every job is essentially custom.

Complex Custom Configurations

Each tool and die reflects a unique customer specification, multiplying the variables tracked from quote to delivery.

Tooling-Wear Tracking Across Sites

Wear patterns on components installed at client sites are hard to monitor without a direct data connection back to the shop.
