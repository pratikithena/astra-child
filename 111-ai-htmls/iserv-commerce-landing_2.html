Consumer commerce assumes one price, one warehouse and one buyer. Industrial commerce assumes none of that. iSERV was built for contract pricing, superseded part numbers, multi-plant stock and the people who actually place the order.

OEMs

Aftermarket revenue is leaking

Customers can't find the right part for a 12-year-old machine, so they buy a copy from someone else. Every lost order takes the service relationship with it.

Serial-number-driven catalogues put the exact part in front of the right customer — and keep the spend on your channel.

Distributors

Quoting eats the margin

Teams rekey enquiries into the ERP, chase stock by phone and quote from a spreadsheet that went stale last quarter. Speed decides the deal, and you're not fast.

Live availability and rule-based pricing return a firm quote in seconds, with zero rekeying.

Interactive 3D views on mobile: tap the component, see stock, order it before leaving the site.

Integration & data3

Native connectors ship for SAP (ECC and S/4HANA), Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Infor, with a documented REST layer for anything else. Sync is bi-directional and covers material master, customer master, contract pricing, ATP/CTP stock, sales orders, deliveries and invoices. Most customers are exchanging live data in the first three weeks of the project.

It's the normal starting point, not a blocker. We import what exists in the ERP and PLM, then use AI-assisted enrichment to deduplicate descriptions, map supersessions and build machine-to-part relationships. You go live with a working catalogue and improve depth in parallel with real orders rather than delaying launch for a perfect dataset.

Your existing CAD. The viewer handles STEP, IGES, JT, SolidWorks, Creo and Inventor files, converting them to a lightweight web format that streams to a browser or phone without a plugin. Hotspots are linked to purchasable line items, so any component a customer can see is a component they can order — including on assemblies you shipped fifteen years ago.

Security & access2

SOC 2 Type II certified, with AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Access is role-based with SSO and SCIM provisioning, every pricing and order action is written to an immutable audit trail, and data residency can be pinned to a specific region. Penetration tests run annually and reports are available under NDA for your security review.

Commercials & rollout2

Your customer pricing stays where it belongs: contract rates, volume breaks, currency and tax resolve per account at request time, either from your ERP condition records or from rules configured in the platform. Our own commercial model is a single annual subscription based on order volume and connected entities, with implementation quoted once and no per-transaction fees.

Six to ten weeks for a standard rollout. Weeks 1–2 cover discovery and ERP connection, weeks 3–6 catalogue import, pricing rules and portal branding, weeks 7–8 user acceptance testing with a pilot dealer group, then go-live and a phased dealer onboarding. You need a product data owner, an ERP contact and a commercial decision-maker — roughly a day a week each.
