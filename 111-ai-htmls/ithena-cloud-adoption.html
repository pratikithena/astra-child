Success Stories

Where cloud modernization helped clients deliver value

Selected success stories where our cloud migration, modernization and optimization services have helped clients scale and cut costs.

Cloud cost optimization

40% reduction in cloud cost for a leading engineering firm

Legacy SCPA systems were ingesting 300 GB a month across 600 pieces of equipment. We cut ingestion to 25 GB a month, modernized SCPA and ThingWorx, and migrated 3 TB of data with minimal disruption – with 32% faster updates.

Scalable platforms

Scalable asset tracking for a U.S. telematics service provider

Legacy modules struggled with 50K+ trailers and 7M+ messages a day. ITHENA’s iSAT delivered scalable provisioning and third-party sensor integration – reaching 100% tracking accuracy and a 45% rise in asset utilization.

Data migration

Data and dashboard migration for an energy management leader

With iRDS we integrated EBS, migrated the client’s data into actionable dashboards and trained their teams – achieving 99% accuracy in migrated data and a 78% increase in productivity within one week.

SaaS & media

Seamless transition to a cloud OTT platform for a leading media company

ITHENA’s iTHEATRE brought cloud storage, content management and DRM together on an independent OTT platform – deployed in 30 days, expanding the user base by 5M+ and growing revenue 15% year on year.

App modernization

5x faster delivery of diagnostic software for an auto engineering provider

We modernized web and mobile diagnostic apps with a dynamic dashboard designer, Agile Scrum, CI/CD and DevOps practices – delivering software 5x faster and boosting revenue by 20%.
