FAQ

Questions about our partnerships

What kinds of partners does ITHENA work with?

Two kinds. Technology partners such as Rockwell Automation, PTC, AWS and Microsoft provide the platforms we build on. Channel partners such as Wesco, EECO and CED are distributors and firms that bring ITHENA solutions to their customers.

Do I need to replace my current software to work with ITHENA?

No. We deploy on the automation, cloud and enterprise platforms you already run, and connect them to persona-based applications for your teams.

What does Gold System Integrator mean?

ITHENA is a Gold System Integrator in the Rockwell Automation partner program. We implement and integrate Rockwell software such as Plex MES, FactoryTalk DataMosaix, Fiix and FactoryTalk Optix.

Can I buy ITHENA through my distributor?

Often, yes. Our channel partners combine their hardware, software and licences with our integration and applications, so you can work through the distributor you already know.

How quickly can a partner-based project deliver value?

Because our applications are pre-built and persona-centric, most customers see value from their partner software in weeks, not months.

How do I become an ITHENA partner?

Use the Become a partner button and tell us about your business. Technology vendors, distributors and consultancies all partner with us.
