In four lines

Social intelligence, explained in four lines.

What it is
iSIP is an AI-powered, web-based middleware that connects to social platforms such as LinkedIn, Google and Facebook, captures your brand's conversations and stores them as structured data for analysis.
Who it is for
Your marketing, customer success, sales, CX and product teams, whenever they need to know how your customers feel about your brand, your products and your competitors, and act on it.
What makes it different
It does not stop at a score. iSIP explains the root cause behind each sentiment and recommends agentic actions that flow into your CRM and marketing tools.
How it rolls out
Configure the jobs that capture each channel, give your teams their access, then switch on the sentiment dashboard, brand search and the recommendations as your data builds.
The challenge

Customer voices are spread across every channel.

Your customers post about your products, installations and support everywhere. Nobody on your team owns it all, so you hear about a shift in perception weeks late.

No single view of sentiment.

Every channel has its own inbox and its own report, so you never see how your customers feel overall, and every team quotes a different number.

Issues surface after they spread.

You spot product, service and brand problems after they have moved across your markets, because your analysts spend days finding patterns by hand.

Blind to your competitors.

You know little about how customers see competing brands, so your positioning and your campaigns rest on a guess rather than on what buyers actually say.

iSIP brings every voice into one live view, explains why it moved, and says what to do next.

FAQ

Social intelligence, answered.

What is iSIP?
ITHENA's Social Intelligence Platform: an AI-powered, web-based middleware that connects to social platforms such as LinkedIn, Google and Facebook, captures your brand's conversations and stores them as structured data for insight and action.
Which channels will iSIP listen to for you?
Social platforms such as LinkedIn, Google and Facebook, plus the industry forums, product reviews and other digital channels where your brand is mentioned.
How fast does your data arrive?
The middleware ingests complex event streams of customer signals every sub-second. You configure a job per channel and monitor its runs in the admin area.
What will the sentiment analysis show you?
Positive, negative and neutral sentiment in real time, the root cause behind it, trends by product, service and topic, and a benchmark of your brand against your competitors.
What are agentic actions?
Recommendations generated from your insights, for engagement, issue resolution, product improvement or campaigns, taken straight into your CRM or marketing tools once a person on your team approves them.
Can you use the data in your own BI tools?
Yes. Your captured mentions are stored as structured data for downstream analysis, so they feed your warehouse and dashboards.
