Our values

Five values. One word: FOCIG.

We put our people at the centre of our business. We serve our employees, customers, partners and society at large with five core values.

01 / 05

Faith

Conviction for our mission

We believe in what we are building and why it matters to you. That conviction keeps us steady through hard problems and long programmes, and it is why we stay until the outcome is real.

We commit to the outcome, not just the task.
02 / 05

Ownership

Accountability for the quality of our work

Every deliverable has an owner who stands behind it. We finish what we start and hold our work to the standard you would expect from your own team.

If it carries our name, we own it.
03 / 05

Curiosity

Innovation with integrity

We keep asking better questions and trying new technology, and we stay honest about what works and what does not, so the ideas we bring you are tested, not just new.

New ideas, tested openly.
04 / 05

Inclusion

Respect and transparency for all

Every voice counts, across roles, offices and backgrounds. We share information openly, so everyone on the team, and on yours, can do their best work.

Open doors, open conversations.
