FAQs

Manufacturing software, answered.

Can't find your question? Ask our team.

Which products are in the Manufacturers suite?

Seven. Planning and production: iPLAN (production planning), iGEMBA (digital Gemba walks), iMEX (manufacturing excellence) and MES (manufacturing execution). Maintenance and quality: CMMS (maintenance management), QMS (quality management) and iSEMS (smart energy).

Do we have to deploy all of them?

No. Each product runs on its own. Most plants start with the lines or assets that hurt most, and each product added later reads the same machines, assets and orders.

Which machines and systems can it connect to?

Machines, HMIs and databases over OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT, Siemens S7 and Euromap 63, and through Ignition where you already run it. Order and calendar data comes from your ERP.

How is iMEX different from an MES?

An MES runs the order from release to finished goods. iMEX sits beside it to make the factory reliable: live OEE, downtime causes, digital logbooks and prediction, so you can act before a loss compounds.

Where does AI fit in?

You ask the plan, the maintenance history or the energy data a question in plain language, with no SQL. iMEX predicts downtime before it happens, and QMS runs AI visual inspection at the station.

How long does go-live take?

Weeks, not quarters. iMEX starts with performance monitoring on the lines that hurt most, and iSEMS goes live in about six weeks.
