FAQs

Enterprise data and AI, answered.

Can't find your question? Ask our team.

Which products are in the Enterprise suite?

Five. Data and AI: Aether (practical AI platform), plant analytics and iHub (enterprise data hub). Enterprise apps: iSIP (social analytics) and iTMS (transportation tracking).

Do we have to move our data anywhere?

No. Aether queries your systems in place, with no warehouse to stand up and no second copy to secure. Only the query and its result travel.

Do we have to replace our ERP, MES or BI tools?

No. iHub sits between the systems you already run, and plant analytics brings its results into the BI tools you use today.

How does the AI avoid a wrong number?

Aether shows its work. The SQL, the tables, the row count and the date range come back with every answer, so a wrong join is visible before anyone acts on it.

Can it run on-premise, and is it secure?

Yes. Your architecture can be cloud, hybrid or on-premise. Credentials are kept apart from data use, access is set per role, and every write is approved and logged.

How fast can we start?

iHub's first connections go live in hours. With Aether, bring one system and last week's questions and you have answers the same afternoon.
