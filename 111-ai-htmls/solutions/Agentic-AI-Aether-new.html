The challenge

The answer arrives after the decision.

Most manufacturers do not have a data problem. They have a queue - every question worth asking has to be written down, prioritised, and turned into a report by the two people who can write SQL.

Every question needs a translator.

Two people who can write SQL sit between forty people and the database. The queue, not the business, decides which questions get asked.

A dashboard is a frozen question.

It answers what someone thought to ask last quarter. The moment a reader asks why the number moved, they are back in the queue.

The number moves and nobody looks.

Scrap on Line 3 crept from 2.1% to 4.8% over six weeks. No alert fired, because no one had thought to write the query that would have caught it.

Knowing and doing sit in different tools.

You learn the seal kit is failing on Tuesday and raise the work order on Friday, in another system, by hand, from a screenshot.

Before you ask

The questions buyers actually put to us.

Answered from what Ithena’s Aether does today, not what is on the roadmap.

Will Ithena’s Aether work with the ERP we already run?

If it has a database or an API, yes. SAP, Oracle, Dynamics, Tally and the usual Indian mid-market systems are connected through read-only credentials. Where an ERP exposes only an API, Ithena’s Aether queries the API and tells you which endpoints it used.

Do we have to move our data anywhere?

No. Ithena’s Aether queries your systems in place. There is no warehouse to stand up, no nightly extract and no second copy of your data to secure or delete later. Only the query and its result travel.

What does it cost?

Per person, per month, with connectors included - not per query, so nobody is discouraged from asking. The thirty-day trial is the whole product, and pricing for your seat count comes back the same day you ask.

What stops it answering with a wrong number?

Nothing stops a wrong question, so Ithena’s Aether shows its work instead: the SQL, the tables, the row count and the date range come back with every answer. If the join is wrong you can see it in one line and correct it. Ambiguous questions are asked back, not guessed at.

Can it write to our systems?

Only where you connect a write path deliberately, and only with an approver. Reads and writes use separate credentials. Every write is queued, shown to a named person, and recorded in the audit log with the question that produced it.

Is our data used to train your models?

No. Your rows, schemas and questions are not used for training, by us or by any model provider we route through. Contractually and technically, your tenant is isolated.
