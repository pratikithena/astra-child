FAQs

OEM and distributor software, answered.

Can't find your question? Ask our team.

Which products are in the OEM and Distributors suite?

Four: iCRM (customer and dealer CRM with CPQ), iERP (build-to-order ERP), iSERV (equipment monitoring and service) and B2B eCommerce (spare parts and service store).

Do we have to buy all four?

No. Each product runs on its own. Start with the one that hurts most. When you add another, it shares the same customer, machine and order records.

Can it connect to our existing ERP?

Yes. An approved iCRM quote becomes the order for your ERP, and the parts store reads live stock, pricing, ATP and CTP from it. REST APIs and webhooks cover the rest.

Where does AI fit in?

The Aether AI assistant answers questions in plain language, flags risk and drafts next steps across the products. It does not send anything on its own: a person approves every draft.

How long does go-live take?

Weeks, not quarters. Most teams start with one product or module and expand once it is running.

Can our customers and dealers use it too?

Yes. iSERV gives your customers a web portal and mobile app for their equipment, and the parts store lets them order, track deliveries and see their history themselves.
