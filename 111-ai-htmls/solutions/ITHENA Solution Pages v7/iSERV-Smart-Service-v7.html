FAQ

Smart service, answered.

Answered from what iSERV does today, not what is on the roadmap.

What is iSERV?

The ITHENA aftermarket service platform for OEMs: machine data, tickets, manuals and spare parts, shared with your customers.

Who uses it?

Your service managers and engineers, and your customers' operators, each with their own view of the same record.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Operators scan a machine's QR code to log a fault, open the manual and order the spare, on iOS and Android.

Can it connect to our ERP?

Yes. Parts lists, stock, price and orders come from the ERP you already run, so nothing is keyed twice.

Does it predict failures?

Yes. Telemetry drives proactive alerts and inspections, so the work order is raised before the stop.

What does Aether add?

Plain-language questions across telemetry, tickets and orders, answered with a chart and an action you approve.
