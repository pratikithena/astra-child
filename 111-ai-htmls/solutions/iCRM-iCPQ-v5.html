FAQ

Industrial CRM and CPQ, answered.

Answered from what iCRM does today, not what is on the roadmap.

What is industrial CPQ?

Configure, price and quote built for equipment. Options carry their own rules, so an invalid machine cannot be quoted, every line rolls into one total and one margin, and a discount below your floor routes to the approver your policy names.

Is CPQ a paid add-on?

No. iCPQ ships in the same product as iCRM, on the same records and permissions. You switch it on when your team is ready, without a second licence or a second system.

How long does it take to go live?

Weeks, not quarters. Most teams start with accounts and the pipeline, add automation and forecasting, then switch on full iCPQ and quote to order. Each phase turns on modules in the same deployment, so nothing is migrated twice.

Does it work inside Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. A sidebar puts the account, the open deal and the next task beside the thread. It resolves the right record from the email, logs the conversation in one click and tracks opens.

Can it connect to our ERP?

Yes. An approved quote becomes the order on the same record, ready for your ERP. Third-party record IDs live in a link table, so the integration can change without your data model changing.

Does the AI send anything on its own?

No. It answers questions, flags deals at risk with the reason and drafts follow-ups, tasks and recaps. Every draft waits for a person to approve or edit it before anything is sent.
