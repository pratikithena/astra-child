In four lines

Transportation tracking, explained in four lines.

What it is
iTMS is the ITHENA transportation application for the Move stage of the supply chain. It gives real-time location, usage and condition of vehicles, consignments and warehouse inventory on persona-driven dashboards.
Who it is for
You, if you run a fleet, a warehouse or a storeroom, or answer for a load in transit: from reefer trucks and medical supplies to secure consignments.
What makes it different
It follows the asset, not just the truck. GPS, RFID, NFC, Bluetooth and LoRa hand a pallet from the storeroom to the dock to the road, so indoor and outdoor tracking are one record.
How it rolls out
Locate, monitor, optimise. Fit devices and see your vehicles live, add condition monitoring and geo-fence alarms, then use utilisation and maintenance data to run fewer, better-used assets.
The challenge

Once it leaves the dock, it becomes a phone call.

With manual tracking, every answer you get is already old. Location comes from a driver, temperature from the arrival check, stock from the last count.

Where is the truck?

Location comes from a driver's call or a logbook, so a delay is discovered when your customer rings, and the answer you give them is already an hour old.

Is the load still cold?

Temperature is checked on arrival. An excursion two hours into the trip is found after the goods are lost, and nobody can say when it started.

Which pallet went where?

Warehouse and storeroom stock is counted, not tracked, so errors travel down the supply chain, and mobile equipment goes missing between sites with no record of use.

iTMS starts at the other end: the asset reports its own position and condition every few seconds, and the alert arrives before the phone call.

FAQ

Transportation tracking, answered.

What does iTMS track?
Your vehicles, the assets they carry, on-site and off-site consignments and warehouse inventory, with real-time location, usage and condition.
Which tracking technologies does it use?
GPS, RFID, NFC, Bluetooth and LoRa, chosen per asset and per site, so your tracking works indoors and outdoors.
How do geo-fence alerts work?
You draw a boundary around a site or zone. When a vehicle or asset enters or leaves it, the event is logged and an SMS or email goes to the person you name.
Can you monitor refrigerated loads?
Yes. Reefer truck tracking gives you real-time monitoring of refrigerated vehicles, with temperature updates at regular intervals and humidity alongside.
Can you track items inside a vehicle?
Yes. Valuable assets are tracked inside the truck, the way bank vehicles track currency in transit, not just the vehicle itself.
What results have operators seen?
A leading logistics company recorded a 25% increase in operational efficiency after fitting IoT-enabled GPS devices. ITHENA asset tracking programmes report 100% tracking accuracy and 45% more asset utilisation.
