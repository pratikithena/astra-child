The challenge

Production you cannot see or trace.

A fast-moving plant needs accurate, real-time data from the shop floor. Without it, losses hide on the line and a single complaint can stop a week of output.

No machine-level view.

Machine performance across lines stays hidden, so you cannot see where efficiency is lost.

Traceability lives on paper.

Compliance and traceability gaps weaken your standards, and every recall question becomes a search.

Scheduling holds back throughput.

Inefficient scheduling leaves lines waiting, while material waste and long cycle times eat into output.

Systems that do not talk.

ERP, machines and quality records sit in separate systems, so valuable business data never comes together.

One complaint, one week, without genealogy

Illustrative, a typical recall query on paper records

Five days and a week of held stock for one faulty batch. With MES, genealogy traces the lot in seconds, so you hold only the batches that share its raw lots.

FAQ

Manufacturing execution, answered.

Answered from what we deliver today.

What is a manufacturing execution system?

A Manufacturing Execution System (MES) is software that monitors and controls complex manufacturing processes. It plans and executes production from order release to finished goods and improves overall production efficiency.

What does ITHENA deliver?

Implementation of MES, real-time visibility into plant floor operations, integration of disparate manufacturing systems, monitoring of multiple plants on a central platform, and multi-location rollouts.

Which MES platforms do you work with?

Our published food manufacturing deployment runs an Ignition-powered Sepasoft MES, integrated with the customer's ERP. We choose the platform with you during requirement elicitation and design.

How do you approach an MES rollout?

In five steps: business understanding, requirement elicitation, design thinking, workshops that identify gaps and bottlenecks, and deployment on proven practices. Each next plant reuses the standard.

Can it handle traceability and compliance?

Yes. Batch-level traceability and genealogy follow every lot across lines, which supports food safety compliance and root cause analysis.

What results have manufacturers seen?

A global packaged food manufacturer recorded 15% higher OEE, 25% better service compliance and traceability, and 12% higher throughput after its MES went live.
