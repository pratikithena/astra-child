In four lines

Maintenance management, explained in four lines.

What it is
A computerised maintenance management system on the ITHENA platform: the asset register, preventive, predictive and breakdown work orders, spares, tools, digital logbooks and checklists, and the maintenance history behind every machine.
Who it is for
Your maintenance managers, planners, technicians and stores, in plants that run production lines, utilities and rotating equipment, and the quality and compliance teams who audit their work.
What makes it different
It is wired to the machines. Work orders come from live condition data and run-hour meters as well as the calendar, so the plan follows what the equipment is actually doing, and the same data feeds OEE and Aether.
How it rolls out
In steps. Register the assets and digitise the logbooks first, then move preventive plans and mobile work orders across, then connect condition data and switch on prediction asset by asset.
The challenge

Maintenance still runs on a clipboard and a memory.

Your plan sits in a spreadsheet, your history sits in a logbook, and the person who knows why the gearbox runs hot is on nights.

You hear about the failure from the line.

Conveyor C2 stops again, someone calls it in, and the motor's last 30 days of readings are nowhere near the job. The repair starts from a guess.

Stores says none, again.

The technician walks to the asset, opens it up and finds the bearing is not in stock. The job waits while the line runs on borrowed time.

Three hours to find one record.

The auditor asks for the press PM record, and someone spends the afternoon in a logbook and a shared drive, rebuilding a story nobody wrote down.

ITHENA CMMS starts at the machine: the asset tells the plan what it needs, and the record writes itself as the work is done.

How it works

One work order, four stages, from the trend to the signed history.

The asset raises the job, the ranking decides what goes first, stores kits it and the crew closes it. Follow one work order, WO-3317 on gearbox GB-4, all the way through. Point at a stage.

1Detect

A failure flagged, not a failure.

The model learns each machine's normal band. When a reading leaves it, a predicted work order is raised against the asset with the trend attached, before the run is interrupted, and the same reading shows on every screen that needs it.

  • Anomaly detection on live machine feeds
  • Work orders from predictions, routed by asset
  • Maintenance windows suggested around production

FAQ

Maintenance management, answered.

What is a CMMS?
A computerised maintenance management system holds your asset register, maintenance plans, work orders, spares and the maintenance history of every machine. ITHENA CMMS adds live machine data, so your work can be triggered by run hours and condition as well as by the calendar.
Which maintenance strategies can you run?
Preventive (calendar and run-hour intervals, including multi-stage intervals), condition-based and predictive maintenance from live data, and breakdown work orders. One asset can carry more than one trigger.
How does it connect to your machines?
Through the ITHENA connectivity layer, which reads OPC UA, Modbus, MQTT, Siemens S7 and Euromap 63, and through Ignition where you already run it. Meter readings and alarms arrive without anyone typing them in.
Can your technicians work from a phone?
Yes. Work orders, checklists, readings and photos are completed on mobile, and the sign-off writes straight to the asset history. Parts are kitted against the job, and tagged tools can be located by RFID, BLE or GPS.
Is your maintenance history audit-ready?
Yes. Every work order, reading, checklist and approval is kept with the user and time in an append-only trail, on a platform that is SOC 2, ISO 27001 and 21 CFR Part 11 ready with GxP validation support.
What results have plants seen?
A craft brewery recorded 30% less unplanned downtime and an industrial boiler provider 35% less, after ITHENA connected their assets and moved maintenance onto digital logbooks, inspections and work orders.
