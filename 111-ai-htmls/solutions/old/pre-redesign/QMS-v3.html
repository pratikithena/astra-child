In four lines

Quality management, explained in four lines.

What it is
Quality management on the ITHENA platform: inline and AI visual inspection, digital SPC, quality gates, NCR and CAPA workflows, digital checklists and travelers, and lot, serial and batch genealogy, with an audit trail on every record.
Who it is for
Your quality engineers, inspectors, line supervisors and QA leads in automotive, pharmaceutical, food, defense and discrete plants, and the auditors and customers they answer to.
What makes it different
It reads the line, not a spreadsheet. Measurements, vision results and machine data arrive live from the station, so SPC, disposition and root cause run on what was actually made, while it is being made.
How it rolls out
In stages. Digitise checklists and inspection points first, connect gauges and cameras for live SPC at the critical stations, then switch on NCR and CAPA workflows, genealogy and AI root cause.
The challenge

Quality found at the end of the line is already scrap.

A defect made at station 2 travels through every station after it, collecting cost, and the shift that made it has gone home by the time anyone sees it.

Defects caught late.

Found at final inspection, after every downstream station has added its cost to the part. Each shift checks differently, and drift is noticed only once parts fail spec.

Paper checklists, missed signatures.

Manual records invite human error, and a missed signature is a compliance gap. Your auditor finds it before you do, and the record cannot be rebuilt after the fact.

Recall scope is a guess.

Materials, products and scrap are not tracked through the cycle, so when one part fails, nobody can say which others share its lot or where they shipped.

ITHENA QMS moves the decision to the station that made the part, while it can still be held.

FAQ

Quality management, answered.

What is a QMS in manufacturing?
A quality management system holds your inspection plans, results, non-conformances, corrective actions and traceability. ITHENA QMS runs them on live line data, so your quality decisions are made at the station while the part can still be held.
How does AI visual inspection work?
Cameras at your station feed computer vision and anomaly detection models that flag surface defects, dimensional deviations and assembly errors in real time, including micro-defects an operator would miss. Results are stored against the part's serial.
Which SPC rules does it apply?
Control charts are built from live measurements per characteristic, with control limits and run rules such as a point beyond the limits, seven points on one side of the centre line or a six-point trend, so drift is flagged before parts fail specification.
How do NCR and CAPA work?
A gate hold raises the non-conformance with the evidence attached. Suspect parts are found through genealogy and quarantined. The CAPA moves through root cause, action and effectiveness check, with each step signed electronically.
Is it suitable for regulated industries?
Yes. The ITHENA platform is SOC 2, ISO 27001 and 21 CFR Part 11 ready, with role-based access, audit trails and GxP validation support. It sits beside your MES and ERP rather than replacing them.
What results have plants seen?
A global Tier 1 automotive supplier recorded a 13% rise in production quality with digital quality assurance and traceability, and a global packaged food manufacturer a 25% increase in compliance and traceability.
