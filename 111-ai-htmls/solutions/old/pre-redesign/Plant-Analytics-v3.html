In four lines

Plant analytics, explained in four lines.

What it is
Analytics built on the ITHENA ONE Data Platform: source connectivity, ingestion, enrichment into a data lake, a semantic layer, end-user visualisation and AI, from the machine signal to the KPI in front of a person.
Who it is for
Your plant and operations leaders, shift supervisors, quality and maintenance teams, and the IT and data teams who today stitch historian, MES and ERP extracts together by hand.
What makes it different
It is persona-based. One model serves every role, so the board's OEE and the operator's OEE are the same number at different depths, and the processing layer correlates non-obvious relationships across sources.
How it rolls out
Six stages: ideate, understand, acquire, build repeatable pipelines, model the data to business requirements, and deploy into production, starting with the plant and KPIs that matter most.
The challenge

The machine reports 91% uptime. It is running at 35.

Availability is the only factor an uptime report carries. Slow running and scrap never reach it, so a machine that lost most of its output gets signed off as healthy.

Your data sits in silos.

Historian tags, MES counts, quality records and ERP orders live in different systems, joined by hand in spreadsheets. Each join is a judgement call, and nobody can trace the figure back to the machine.

Reports arrive after the shift.

By the time an export is cleaned and charted, the loss it describes has already happened again. Your supervisors manage yesterday's line with last week's numbers.

Nobody trusts the number.

Your departments compute OEE two ways, and the meeting is spent arguing about the figure instead of the line. The board sees one number and the floor sees another.

ITHENA starts at the other end: model the data once, and every role reads the same KPI at its own depth.

FAQ

Plant analytics, answered.

What is the ONE Data Platform?
ITHENA's framework that carries your data from the source connection to insight in front of your people: source connectivity, ingestion, enrichment into a data lake, a semantic layer, end-user visualisation and AI.
Which sources can you connect?
Industrial assets, sensors and devices through pre-built connectors and drivers, plus historians, MES, quality records, ERP, CRM, telematics and cloud applications. Older machines are reached through gateways.
What is NORA?
Non-obvious relationship attributes. The Intelligent Processing Layer correlates data across your machines, people and sub-systems to surface relationships nobody thought to chart, such as a quality loss that tracks a condition recorded in another system.
Do you need to replace your BI tools?
No. ITHENA brings machine learning outcomes into the BI tools you use, and migrates BI platforms where that is your goal. Enterprise BI, self-service frameworks and mobile BI are all part of the offering.
Can it run on-premise, and is it secure?
Yes. Your architecture can be cloud, hybrid or on-premise. Credential management is kept apart from data usage, access is set per role, and security and audit run end to end.
What results have clients seen?
A leading agribusiness recorded 42% higher reliability of OEE and 36% less manual reporting effort, and a U.S. steel pails manufacturer recorded 96% faster reporting and 23% savings on inventory costs.
