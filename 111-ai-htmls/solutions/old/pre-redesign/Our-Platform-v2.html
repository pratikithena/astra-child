    FAQ

    Questions about the platform

    What is the ITHENA platform?

    A generative AI persona platform for industrial businesses. It brings Industrial IoT, enterprise data assets and big data together, and turns them into role-specific outcomes across production, service and supply chain.

    What are the four layers?

    Edge and universe connectivity connects your assets and systems. The data ingestion layer holds volume, velocity and variety in one place. The intelligent processing layer aggregates, integrates, models and derives. The experience layer renders the result for your role.

    What is NORA?

    Non-obvious relationship attributes. The intelligent processing layer correlates data from machines, tags, people and systems to find relationships your teams would not think to check, beyond what is obvious from human interaction.

    Which systems can it connect to?

    Most enterprise, cloud and industrial assets, sensors and devices, out of the box. Prebuilt connectors and drivers cover machines and PLCs, historians, ERP and MES, cloud apps and devices, so you are not starting a custom integration project.

    How quickly will we see results?

    In weeks. The platform was conceived around role-specific outcomes, so you start with one role and one outcome, measure it, then extend to the next on the same data.

    Where does generative AI come in?

    In the experience layer. You ask in natural language and it renders structured or ad-hoc analysis for your role, with the next action, instead of a generic dashboard.
