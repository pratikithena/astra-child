FAQ

Manufacturing execution, answered.

Answered from what we deliver today.

What is a manufacturing execution system?

Software that runs production from order release to finished goods, and tracks every step on the way.

What does ITHENA deliver?

MES implementation, plant-floor visibility, system integration and multi-location rollouts.

Which MES platforms do you work with?

Our published food deployment runs Sepasoft MES on Ignition. We choose the platform with you during design.

How do you approach a rollout?

Understand, run workshops, design, deploy. Each next plant reuses the standard set by the first.

Can it handle traceability and compliance?

Yes. Batch-level genealogy follows every lot, for food safety compliance and root cause analysis.

Does it connect to our ERP?

Yes. Orders are released from the ERP, and results are posted back as finished goods.
