FAQ

Industrial CRM and CPQ, answered.

Answered from what iCRM does today, not what is on the roadmap.

What is industrial CPQ?

Configure, price and quote built for equipment. Rules block invalid options, and every line rolls into one total and one margin.

Is CPQ a paid add-on?

No. iCPQ ships with iCRM, on the same records. Switch it on when your team is ready.

How long does it take to go live?

Weeks, not quarters. Most teams start with the pipeline, then add forecasting and CPQ.

Does it work inside Gmail and Outlook?

Yes. A sidebar shows the account, deal and next task beside the thread, and logs the email.

Can it connect to our ERP?

Yes. An approved quote becomes the order, ready for your ERP.

Does the AI send anything on its own?

No. It answers, flags and drafts. A person approves every draft before it goes out.
