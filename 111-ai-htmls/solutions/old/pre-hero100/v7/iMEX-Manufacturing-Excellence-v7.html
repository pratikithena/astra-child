FAQ

Manufacturing excellence, answered.

Answered from what iMEX does today, not what is on the roadmap.

What is iMEX?

The ITHENA Industrial IoT platform for manufacturing excellence: live OEE and SQDP on every line, and AI that predicts the stop.

Which machines can it connect to?

Machines, HMIs, databases and systems of record, over OPC UA, Euromap 63 and other standard protocols.

How is iMEX different from an MES?

An MES runs the order. iMEX sits beside it to make the plant reliable: OEE, downtime causes, digital checks and prediction.

How long does it take to deploy?

Weeks. Most plants start with performance monitoring on the lines that hurt most.

What does FactoryGPT add?

Plain-language answers on shift metrics, continuous anomaly scanning, and work orders drafted before the loss compounds.

Does it replace paper checks?

Yes. Inspections and logbooks run on the operator's phone, with overdue checks on top.
