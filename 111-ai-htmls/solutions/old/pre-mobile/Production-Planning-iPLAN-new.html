In four lines

Production and supply chain planning, explained in four lines.

What it is
iPLAN is the ITHENA advanced planning and scheduling solution, powered by Ortems. It builds a finite-capacity production schedule, balances demand against supply and material, and slots maintenance into the plan.
Who it is for
You plan or schedule production, run supply and materials, or answer for delivery dates, across one plant or many.
What makes it different
Your planning and execution are joined. The schedule is built from your ERP orders and PLM routings, released to your MES and machines, and re-planned from what your floor actually did.
How it rolls out
In phases. Workshops on how you plan today, then visibility on the schedule, then optimisation, then synchronisation with your suppliers and shop floor, with multi-plant rollouts and 24x7 support behind you.
The challenge

The plan is right on Monday. By Wednesday it is fiction.

Your plan is built once a week in a spreadsheet, against infinite capacity, and then your floor improvises. Nobody sees the drift until you miss a delivery date.

Your plan assumes infinite capacity.

A weekly spreadsheet books two jobs on one spindle and a shift nobody is working. It looks feasible on paper, fails on the floor, and has no answer when MTO and ETO variety arrives.

Planning and the floor are out of sync.

Silos hide what other teams are doing. The floor improvises, actuals never flow back, and the next plan starts from what should have happened instead of what did.

A disruption reaches you as a missed date.

A rush order, a breakdown or a late supplier ripples through every order behind it. Without a real-time view you find out when your customer does.

iPLAN starts at the other end: a plan that re-plans itself against your real capacity, your real material and what your floor really did.

FAQ

Planning and scheduling, answered.

What is iPLAN?
iPLAN is the ITHENA advanced planning and scheduling solution, powered by Ortems. It builds your finite-capacity production schedule, balances demand against supply and material, and schedules maintenance, for one plant or many.
How is production planning different from supply chain planning?
Production planning works inside your plant: capacity, sequence, material release and tracking. Supply chain planning works across your network: demand forecasts, S&OP, inventory and supplier deliveries. In iPLAN both read the same orders, capacities and stock.
What is finite-capacity scheduling?
A schedule that treats your machines, crews and tools as real limits with their own calendars, so it never books more work on a resource than it can do. Infinite-capacity plans look feasible on paper and fail on the floor.
What happens when a rush order or a breakdown hits?
iPLAN re-plans. Operations move to the next capable resource or the next free slot, and you see which orders the change affects, and by how much, before you accept it. Preventive maintenance is placed in planned gaps the same way.
Do you have to replace your ERP or MES?
No. iPLAN sits between them. Orders, bills of material and stock come from your ERP, routings from your PLM, schedules go to your MES, and actuals return from your MES and machines.
What results have manufacturers seen?
A US defense manufacturer recorded a 27% reduction in lead time, a 29% decrease in downtime and 4x faster delivery after iPLAN took over its planning and scheduling.
