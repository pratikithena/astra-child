FAQ

Manufacturing excellence, answered.

Answered from what iMEX does today, not what is on the roadmap.

What is iMEX?

iMEX is the ITHENA Industrial IoT platform for manufacturing excellence. It connects your machines and systems, shows live OEE and SQDP on persona-based dashboards, and uses an AI/ML engine to predict and prevent downtime.

Which machines and systems can it connect to?

Machines, databases, HMIs and systems of record, over standard protocols such as OPC UA and Euromap 63. It also reads LMS and CMMS data, so man, methods, machines and measures sit in one view.

How is iMEX different from an MES?

An MES runs the order from release to finished goods. iMEX sits beside it to make the factory reliable: live OEE, downtime causes, digital logbooks and prediction, so you can act before a loss compounds.

How long does it take to deploy?

Weeks. iMEX is lightweight and grows with your use cases, and customers report 30% less deployment time. Most plants start with performance monitoring on the lines that hurt most.

What does FactoryGPT add?

Natural-language questions on shift metrics and downtime, continuous anomaly scanning on throughput and cycle time, and corrective work orders raised before losses compound.

What results have manufacturers seen?

A U.S. cabinet manufacturer running 16 plants recorded 48% higher machine availability, 30% less unplanned downtime and two hours saved per operator shift.
