iSERV Capabilities

Self-Servicing at Your Fingertips.

iSERV enables the various roles at your customers, including service technicians, managers and other team members, with digital, real-time visibility of equipment information. Operators or technicians can log cases and collaborate with the OEM’s service team, all from an integrated, easy-to-use mobile application and a web portal.

It gives the OEM’s service team the ability to connect, visualize and act on the customer’s issues within the defined Service Level Agreements (SLA).
