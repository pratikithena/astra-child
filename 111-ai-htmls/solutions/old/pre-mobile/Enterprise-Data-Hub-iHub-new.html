In four lines

An enterprise data hub, explained in four lines.

What it is
iHub is the data layer of the ITHENA platform: edge connectivity, ingestion, a canonical manufacturing model and an event bus that moves data both ways between operational technology and business systems.
Who it is for
You, if you run more than one plant system and your IT, OT and operations teams still move data between them by hand or by spreadsheet.
What makes it different
It is built for manufacturing, not bolted on. Planning, production, quality, safety, delivery and maintenance land in one model, and prebuilt playbooks such as alarm to work order act on it.
How it rolls out
A configuration wizard discovers your stack, tests connections, selects tables and defines joins, live in hours. Prove it on one line, then scale it across sites, cloud-native with an optional edge.
The challenge

Your factory runs on disconnected islands.

Every one of your systems generates data, and almost none of it reaches the next. You pay for it in delayed decisions, missed insights and manual workarounds, every shift.

Your ERP cannot see the line.

You plan orders, schedules and bills of material against a plant your ERP only hears about at shift end, when someone types it in. By then the plan is already wrong.

Alarms never become work orders.

The historian records the fault in milliseconds. Your CMMS learns about it when a technician remembers to raise the ticket, and the gap between the two is downtime.

Silos are bridged with spreadsheets.

Every report you read is a manual export and a join somebody maintains by hand, so it arrives late, breaks when a source changes, and no two reports agree.

iHub sits between the two floors: one bus, one model, and every event routed to the system that has to act.

FAQ

Enterprise data hub, answered.

What is an enterprise data hub for manufacturing?
A layer between your shop-floor operational technology and your top-floor business systems that exchanges data both ways. iHub connects your SCADA, MES, historians, IoT, ERP, CMMS and quality systems, models their data once, and routes each event to the system that has to act.
Which of your systems can iHub connect to?
ERP, maintenance, quality, safety and analytics platforms on your top floor; SCADA, HMI, MES, historians, IoT, PLCs, RTUs, sensors, barcode, RFID and vision on your shop floor. Protocols include OPC UA, MQTT, Kafka, REST, JDBC/SQL and files.
What is the canonical data model?
One normalised model for your assets, tags, events, production, quality and inventory. Every source maps onto it, so your dashboards, playbooks and AI read the same record whichever system produced the data.
How long does it take to connect a plant?
The configuration wizard discovers your stack, tests connections, selects tables and defines joins, so your first connections go live in hours. A full rollout runs assessment, plan, proof of value, pilot and scale.
Do you have to replace your MES or ERP?
No. iHub sits between the systems you already run, and adapters can change without changing the canonical model, so nothing that reads from it breaks.
How is your shop floor kept secure?
Multi-tenant role-based access, SSO and OIDC, a secrets vault, audit logs and OT network isolation are part of the platform, with optional edge components that keep OT traffic local.
