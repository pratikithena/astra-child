In four lines

Smart energy management, explained in four lines.

What it is
iSEMS is the ITHENA smart, modular energy information and management application. Powered by IoT and AI-based analytics, it monitors, reports and optimises Water, Air, Gas, Electricity and Steam.
Who it is for
You, if you run energy, a plant or sustainability in an industrial or commercial organisation and need to cut energy cost, carbon and compliance risk together.
What makes it different
It bridges monitoring and consultation. The software reads every asset, and the ITHENA team runs the audit, sets your targets and builds your sustainability roadmap with you.
How it rolls out
Audit, set, plan, measure, run. Deployed end to end in about six weeks, from the first meter to live dashboards and reports, then supported as tariffs and targets change.
The challenge

The energy bill is one number. The waste hides inside it.

Your monthly total tells you what you spent. It does not tell you which asset spent it, which unit of output it went into, or what it did to your carbon figure.

Nobody knows which asset drew it.

Consumption arrives as a site total, so the biggest loads are guessed at instead of measured, and every saving plan starts with an argument about where the energy went.

A leak looks like normal use.

Without a meter where air, gas and steam actually run, consumption that no asset asked for never shows up. Meanwhile harmonics heat your transformers long before anyone sees a reading.

Emissions are estimated, not calculated.

Scope 1, 2 and 3 are rebuilt from bills once a year. The figure takes weeks to assemble, and it does not survive the first question an auditor asks.

iSEMS starts at the other end: a meter on every asset that matters, read live, reported by line, and turned into a carbon number you can defend.

FAQ

Smart energy management, answered.

What is iSEMS?
iSEMS is ITHENA's smart energy management solution. It monitors and reports energy usage across your facility, with a drilled-down view of each asset, so you save on energy cost, cut your carbon footprint and stay compliant.
What does W.A.G.E.S mean?
Water, Air, Gas, Electricity and Steam. iSEMS helps you control and optimise all five, read per asset rather than per bill.
How does iSEMS reduce your operating cost?
By finding energy wastage asset by asset so you can stop it. iSEMS programmes target a 30% reduction in operational costs.
Can you see energy cost per unit produced?
Yes. Detailed energy reports correlate the units you produce with the energy you use, down to the energy cost of each unit, line by line.
How are Scope 1, 2 and 3 calculated?
From the data iSEMS already collects: Scope 1 from diesel combustion and fugitive emissions, Scope 2 from power consumption including PPAs and alternative sources, Scope 3 from raw materials, manufacturing, packaging, storage and end of life.
How long until you are live?
About six weeks end to end, from kick-off to live dashboards and reports, with ITHENA supporting the audit, targets and roadmap alongside the software.
