In four lines

Digital Gemba, explained in four lines.

What it is
iGEMBA is ITHENA's digital Gemba walk tool. It replaces paper checklists and whiteboards with configurable checklists, data input screens, a single view of department KPIs, Kanban action tracking and analytics.
Who it is for
Your supervisors and team leads who run the daily walk and huddle, the department heads who own your KPIs, and plant leaders who need every department's performance in one place.
What makes it different
The history stays. Every daily value is kept, so the board that runs today's huddle is also the record you analyse for trends, patterns and root cause next month.
How it rolls out
Department by department. Configure the checklist, KPI formulae and colour limits, run the walk digitally, add analytics, then take the next step into iMEX when machines need connecting.
The challenge

Paper Gemba walks forget everything by Friday.

At best a traditional walk creates a long trail of paperwork that takes hours to manage. At worst the numbers never leave the clipboard.

No history to learn from.

Last month's values live in a binder, so nobody can trend a KPI, spot that the same line stops every Wednesday, or test whether a fix actually worked.

Hours lost to paperwork.

Sheets are filled, collected, retyped and filed. The walk becomes an admin task, and leaders see a department's numbers only when they stand in front of its board.

Actions with no owner.

"Ask maintenance" is written in the margin, and a week later nobody can say whether it was done, who did it, or whether the problem came back.

iGEMBA keeps the walk and loses the paper: the same numbers, coloured, kept, and acted on.

FAQ

Digital Gemba, answered.

What is a digital Gemba walk?
A Gemba walk means going to where the work happens to observe and improve it, a practice rooted in Kaizen. A digital walk records your checklist, KPI values and actions in software instead of on paper, so you keep them and can analyse them.
Can you define your own KPIs and limits?
Yes. You configure KPI formulae and colour limits per department, so each KPI is scored and coloured the way your plant defines it.
How are actions from the huddle tracked?
On Kanban boards. Your corrective actions, issues and plans get an owner and are tracked through to done, with real-time visibility for everyone on the board.
What does the analytics feature give you?
Historical data in a single frame, daily-level insight into KPI performance, trends and patterns, in-depth root cause analysis of issues, and the daily values of any KPI for a month you choose.
How does iGEMBA relate to iMEX?
iGEMBA digitises your walk and the KPIs your people record. iMEX is the next step: it connects your machines for real-time visibility, workflows, reporting and alerting.
What results have plants seen?
A global battery manufacturer cut issue resolution time by 26%. Other iGEMBA plants recorded 25% faster decision-making, 32% faster issue resolution and 32% higher productivity.
