01 / 04Edge & Universe Connectivity

The perceptron layer

Connect | Collect | Visualize: it starts here. With pre-built connectors and drivers, the platform connects to most enterprise, cloud, industrial assets, sensors, and devices: out-of-the-box.

ITHENA’s Smart Insights platform empowers users to delve deep into understanding their workforce, processes, and content, thereby facilitating business transformation. Through this platform, you obtain a real-time glimpse into your process performance and the content that propels them. Consequently, this equips you to enhance customer experiences, fortify competitive standing, amplify visibility, and ensure compliance.

02 / 04Data Ingestion Layer

Velocity | Volume | Variety of data in one place

Housing relevant information by Volume | Velocity | Variety: our integrated data layer ensures the sanctity across sources & destinations.

Bringing in data across multiple domains including Complex Event Streams | Telemetry Data | Enterprise Systems of Records | Big Data, our applications bring the best user experience for THAT role.

03 / 04Intelligent Processing Layer

Driven by Human & Artificial Intelligence

The aggregation | integration | modeling | derivation of all aspects of what users should experience is built in this IPL to enable visual outcomes by personas.

The Intelligent Processing Layer serves as the conduit between the varied data sources housed in the Data Layer. This layer enhances the value of data that is brought in through connected machines, TAGs, people and other sub-systems, for eventually providing to the Experience Layer for intelligent industrial outcomes. Behind the scenes, the IPL achieves this ease by modeling, aggregating, deriving, and correlating numerous factors of disparate data.

04 / 04Experience Layer

Role-centric application workflow

Where users engage, interact and are driven by generative AI based rendering of structured, ad-hoc analysis using computer and natural language processing for their roles.

One of the distinguishing features of the ITHENA platform is the persona or role-centricity. Driven by a combination of human and artificial intelligence, we provide analytical outcomes to the relevant persona, and not just the art-of-the-possible scenarios! Our applications bring the best user experience for THAT role, driven by actionable workflows and machine-learned intelligence.
